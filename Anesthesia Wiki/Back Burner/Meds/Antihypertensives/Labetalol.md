# Labetalol

## Quick Facts
- Generic: Labetalol
- Brand: Trandate
- Class: Mixed alpha-1 and nonselective beta blocker
- Controlled substance: No
- Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Indication-Specific Guidance

### 1) Perioperative Hypertension / Tachycardia
#### Dosing
- 5 to 20 mg IV bolus, repeat every 10 minutes as needed
- Infusion protocols vary by institution
#### Practical Considerations
- Useful when both BP and HR are elevated
- Longer duration than esmolol

## Onset and Duration
- Onset: 2–5 minutes IV
- Peak: 5–15 minutes
- Duration: 2–4 hours

## Mechanism of Action
- Alpha-1 blockade reduces SVR
- Beta-1 blockade reduces HR and contractility
- Beta-2 blockade may promote bronchospasm in susceptible patients

## Contraindications / Avoid
- Severe bradycardia
- High-grade AV block without pacing
- Cardiogenic shock/decompensated heart failure
- Severe reactive airway disease (relative)

## Key Warnings and Cautions
- Can precipitate bronchospasm
- Avoid aggressive dosing in preload-dependent physiology

## Common Adverse Effects
- Bradycardia
- Hypotension
- Dizziness/fatigue

## Monitoring
- Continuous BP and ECG
- HR trend after each bolus

## Important Interactions
- Additive bradycardia with calcium channel blockers and other AV nodal blockers

## Notes for Local Customization
- Frequently used in OB and general perioperative settings for episodic severe hypertension.
