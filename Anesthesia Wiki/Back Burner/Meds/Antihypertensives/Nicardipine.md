# Nicardipine

## Quick Facts
- Generic: Nicardipine
- Brand: Cardene
- Class: Dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker
- Controlled substance: No
- Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Indication-Specific Guidance

### 1) Perioperative Hypertension (Titrated Control)
#### Dosing
- Start 2.5 to 5 mg/hr IV infusion
- Titrate by 1 to 2.5 mg/hr every 5–15 minutes
- Typical range 2.5 to 15 mg/hr
#### Practical Considerations
- Good arterial vasodilator with relatively predictable titration
- Useful for neuro cases requiring smooth BP control

## Onset and Duration
- Onset: 5–10 minutes
- Duration: 30 minutes to several hours depending on infusion duration

## Mechanism of Action
- Blocks L-type calcium channels in vascular smooth muscle, producing arterial vasodilation and reduced afterload

## Contraindications / Avoid
- Advanced aortic stenosis (relative)
- Hypotension

## Key Warnings and Cautions
- Reflex tachycardia can occur
- Can increase intracranial blood volume in select neuro contexts (coordinate with surgical goals)

## Common Adverse Effects
- Headache
- Flushing
- Reflex tachycardia
- Peripheral edema (longer infusions)

## Monitoring
- Continuous arterial or frequent noninvasive BP
- HR monitoring during titration

## Important Interactions
- Additive hypotension with other vasodilators

## Notes for Local Customization
- Often chosen when fine BP titration is preferred over intermittent boluses.
