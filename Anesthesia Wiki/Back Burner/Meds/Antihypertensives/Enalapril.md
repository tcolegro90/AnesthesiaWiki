# Enalaprilat

## Quick Facts
- Generic: Enalaprilat (IV active metabolite of enalapril)
- Brand: Vasotec IV
- Class: ACE inhibitor
- Controlled substance: No
- Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Indication-Specific Guidance

### 1) Perioperative Hypertension (Selected Cases)
#### Dosing
- 0.625 to 1.25 mg IV over 5 minutes
- May repeat every 6 hours; max usually 5 mg per dose
#### Practical Considerations
- Slower onset than many alternatives
- Can be useful for sustained control rather than immediate beat-to-beat titration

## Onset and Duration
- Onset: 15 minutes
- Peak: 1–4 hours
- Duration: approximately 6 hours

## Mechanism of Action
- Inhibits angiotensin-converting enzyme, reducing angiotensin II and aldosterone effects

## Contraindications / Avoid
- Pregnancy
- Bilateral renal artery stenosis
- Prior ACE inhibitor angioedema
- Hyperkalemia

## Key Warnings and Cautions
- Hypotension risk, especially volume-depleted patients
- May worsen renal function in susceptible patients

## Common Adverse Effects
- Hypotension
- Cough (less relevant acute IV setting)
- Hyperkalemia

## Monitoring
- BP response
- Renal function and potassium when repeated dosing is used

## Important Interactions
- Potassium-sparing agents increase hyperkalemia risk
- Additive hypotension with other antihypertensives
