# Sodium Nitroprusside

## Quick Facts
- Generic: Sodium nitroprusside
- Brand: Nitropress
- Class: Direct arterial and venous vasodilator (nitric oxide donor)
- Controlled substance: No
- Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Indication-Specific Guidance

### 1) Hypertensive Emergency / Controlled Hypotension
#### Dosing
- Start 0.3 mcg/kg/min IV infusion
- Titrate typically up to 10 mcg/kg/min (short periods at higher doses)
#### Practical Considerations
- Very rapid onset/offset allows precise control
- Prefer short durations and lowest effective doses

## Onset and Duration
- Onset: seconds
- Offset: 1–2 minutes after discontinuation

## Mechanism of Action
- Releases nitric oxide causing smooth muscle relaxation in both arterial and venous beds

## Contraindications / Avoid
- Inadequate cerebral perfusion states where abrupt BP drop is dangerous
- Severe renal/hepatic failure (relative, due to metabolite handling)

## Key Warnings and Cautions
- Cyanide and thiocyanate toxicity risk with high doses/prolonged infusion
- Can cause coronary steal in ischemic heart disease
- Protect solution from light per product guidance

## Common Adverse Effects
- Hypotension
- Reflex tachycardia
- Nausea/headache

## Monitoring
- Continuous arterial BP preferred
- Acid-base status and lactate if prolonged/high-dose use
- Toxicity monitoring in prolonged infusions

## Important Interactions
- Additive hypotension with other vasodilators and anesthetics

## Notes for Local Customization
- Reserve for settings where minute-to-minute BP control and invasive monitoring are available.
