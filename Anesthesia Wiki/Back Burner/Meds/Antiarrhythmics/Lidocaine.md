# Lidocaine (Antiarrhythmic)

## Quick Facts
- Generic: Lidocaine
- Brand: Xylocaine
- Class: Class Ib antiarrhythmic (sodium channel blocker)
- Controlled substance: No
- Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Indication-Specific Guidance

### 1) Ventricular Arrhythmias (VT/VF alternative to amiodarone)
#### Dosing
- 1 to 1.5 mg/kg IV bolus
- Additional 0.5 to 0.75 mg/kg every 5–10 minutes (max cumulative dose typically 3 mg/kg)
- Infusion: 1 to 4 mg/min

## Onset and Duration
- Onset: rapid (seconds to minutes)
- Duration: 10–20 minutes after bolus

## Mechanism of Action
- Blocks inactivated fast sodium channels in ventricular myocardium, shortening action potential in ischemic tissue

## Contraindications / Avoid
- Severe SA/AV conduction disease without pacing
- Known amide local anesthetic hypersensitivity (rare)

## Key Warnings and Cautions
- CNS toxicity risk with high serum levels (tinnitus, perioral numbness, seizures)
- Dose reduction may be needed in severe hepatic dysfunction or low cardiac output

## Common Adverse Effects
- Drowsiness
- Paresthesias
- Tremor/confusion
- Bradycardia/hypotension at high levels

## Monitoring
- ECG rhythm response
- Neurologic signs of systemic toxicity

## Important Interactions
- Additive cardiac/CNS effects with other sodium channel blockers
