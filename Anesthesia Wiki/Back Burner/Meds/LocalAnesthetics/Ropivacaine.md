# Ropivacaine

## Quick Facts
- Generic: Ropivacaine
- Class: Amide local anesthetic
- Typical concentrations: 0.2%, 0.5%, 0.75%, 1%
- Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Indication-Specific Guidance

### 1) Local Infiltration / Field Block
#### Typical Dosing
- Common reference: up to about 3 mg/kg (often max 225 mg)
#### Practical Considerations
- Often selected for long duration with relatively favorable motor-sparing profile at lower concentrations.

### 2) Peripheral Nerve Block
#### Typical Dosing
- Concentration and volume vary by block type.
- Keep cumulative mg below weight-based and institutional maximums.
#### Practical Considerations
- Useful for prolonged analgesia strategies.

### 3) Epidural Analgesia / Anesthesia
#### Typical Dosing
- Institutional protocol dependent for bolus and infusion.
#### Practical Considerations
- Track cumulative dose from all bolus and infusion components.

## Onset and Duration
- Onset: intermediate
- Duration: long

## Contraindications / Avoid
- Known amide local anesthetic allergy (rare)

## Key Warnings and Cautions
- LAST remains possible despite improved cardiovascular profile versus bupivacaine.
- Dose calculations should include all local anesthetics administered.

## Monitoring
- Standard regional anesthesia monitoring
- Ongoing reassessment for delayed toxicity signs

## Notes for Local Customization
- Maintain block-specific concentration/volume defaults per local practice.
