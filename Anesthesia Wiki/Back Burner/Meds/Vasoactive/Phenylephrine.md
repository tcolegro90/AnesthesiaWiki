# Phenylephrine

## Quick Facts
- Generic: Phenylephrine
- Brand: Neo-Synephrine (and others)
- Class: Pure alpha-1 agonist vasopressor
- Controlled substance: No
- Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Indication-Specific Guidance

### 1) Perioperative Hypotension (especially vasodilation)
#### Dosing
- Bolus: 40 to 200 mcg IV
- Infusion: 0.2 to 2 mcg/kg/min or 20 to 200 mcg/min (institution-dependent)
#### Practical Considerations
- Increases SVR with minimal direct chronotropy/inotropy
- Often useful when tachycardia is present

## Onset and Duration
- Onset: immediate IV
- Duration: short (minutes)

## Mechanism of Action
- Selective alpha-1 receptor agonist causing arterial and venous vasoconstriction

## Contraindications / Avoid
- Relative caution in severe bradycardia/low cardiac output states

## Key Warnings and Cautions
- Reflex bradycardia can worsen low CO physiology
- Tissue injury risk with extravasation

## Common Adverse Effects
- Hypertension
- Reflex bradycardia
- Reduced tissue perfusion at high doses

## Monitoring
- Continuous BP and HR

## Important Interactions
- Additive vasoconstriction with other sympathomimetics

## Notes for Local Customization
- Widely used first-line bolus vasopressor in the OR.
