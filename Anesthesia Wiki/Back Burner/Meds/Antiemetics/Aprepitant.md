# Aprepitant (Emend)

## Quick Facts
- Generic: Aprepitant
- Brand: Emend
- Class: Neurokinin-1 (NK₁) receptor antagonist / antiemetic
- Controlled substance: No
- Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Indication-Specific Guidance

### 1) PONV Prophylaxis (High-Risk Patients)
#### Dosing
- 40 mg PO administered 1 to 3 hours before induction
#### Practical Considerations
- Long duration makes it effective for **post-discharge nausea and vomiting (PDNV)** — similar advantage to scopolamine
- Most effective as part of a multimodal combination PONV protocol

### 2) Chemotherapy-Induced Nausea/Vomiting (CINV)
#### Dosing
- 125 mg PO day 1, then 80 mg PO days 2 and 3 (per oncology protocol)

## Onset and Duration
- Onset: oral — effect begins within 1–2 hours
- Duration: > 24–40 hours (long-acting)

## Mechanism of Action
- Selective **NK₁ (Neurokinin-1) receptor antagonist** — blocks the action of substance P at the NK₁ receptor in the central nervous system (area postrema and nucleus tractus solitarius)
- Complementary mechanism to 5-HT₃ antagonists and steroids

## Contraindications / Avoid
- Known hypersensitivity
- Concurrent use of pimozide, terfenadine, astemizole, cisapride (CYP3A4-mediated interactions)

## Key Warnings and Cautions
- **CYP3A4 inhibitor**: Aprepitant inhibits CYP3A4 and may increase plasma concentrations of drugs metabolized by this pathway (e.g., dexamethasone — reduce dexamethasone dose by ~50% when combined; certain immunosuppressants requiring close monitoring)
- **CYP2C9 inducer**: May reduce efficacy of warfarin — monitor INR

## Common Adverse Effects
- Fatigue
- Hiccups
- Constipation
- Elevated LFTs (transient)

## Monitoring
- No specific monitoring in surgical prophylaxis context
- Warfarin INR if concurrent anticoagulation

## Important Interactions
- **Dexamethasone**: CYP3A4 inhibition increases dexamethasone levels — reduce dexamethasone dose by approximately 50% when combining
- **Warfarin**: Reduced efficacy — monitor INR
- **Oral contraceptives**: May reduce contraceptive efficacy — use backup method for 1 month

## Notes for Local Customization
- Often added to the antiemetic protocol for high APFEL score patients (3–4 risk factors).
