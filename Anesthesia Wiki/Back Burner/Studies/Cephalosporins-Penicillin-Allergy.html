Safely using cephalosporins in almost all patients with penicillin allergies

Gynecologic Oncology Reports, Volume 53 (2024), Article 101389

Implications

Most penicillin-allergy labels are low risk, so cefazolin is usually safe to give and supports better prophylaxis outcomes than routine alternative agents.

Brief Summary

Across the reviewed literature, using cefazolin in most patients with low-risk reported penicillin allergy was associated with better prophylaxis performance, including lower surgical site infection rates, fewer adverse effects tied to broader-spectrum alternatives (including less acute kidney injury), improved antimicrobial stewardship, and overall cost savings.
