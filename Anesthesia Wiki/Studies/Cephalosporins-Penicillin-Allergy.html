Safely using cephalosporins in almost all patients with penicillin allergies

Gynecologic Oncology Reports, Volume 53 (2024), Article 101389

Brief Summary

This mini-review supports protocol-based cefazolin use in most patients who report penicillin allergy. The paper highlights that many penicillin-allergy labels do not represent true high-risk cross-reactivity with cefazolin, and that using cefazolin when appropriate may improve antimicrobial stewardship, lower surgical site infection risk, avoid kidney injury seen with some alternatives, and reduce cost.
