Medication Colors Reference

Standard color stickers for anesthesia medications (ASTM/ISO 26825)

Opioids Morphine, Fentanyl, Hydromorphone, etc.
Neuromuscular Blockers Succinylcholine, Rocuronium, Vecuronium, etc.
Induction Agents Propofol, Etomidate, Ketamine, etc.
Benzodiazepines Diazepam, Midazolam, Lorazepam, etc.
Local Anesthetics Lidocaine, Bupivacaine, Ropivacaine, etc.
Vasopressors Epinephrine, Phenylephrine, Norepinephrine, etc.
Anticholinergics Atropine, Glycopyrrolate, Scopolamine, etc.
