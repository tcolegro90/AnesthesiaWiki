Malignant Hyperthermia

1. Recognition (Clinical Events)

Sudden rise in etCO2 (>55 mmHg)
Unexplained tachycardia
Masseter/generalized rigidity
Rising patient temperature
Cola-colored urine (myoglobinuria)
Mottled or cyanotic skin
Decreased SpO2
Labile blood pressure
Labs: Resp/Metabolic Acidosis, serum K > 6 mEq/L, Creatine Kinase > 20,000 u/L, serum/urine myoglobin.

2. Treatment (Action Plan)

1
Call for help / Stop surgery
2
Discontinue volatiles / Succinylcholine
3
Administer Dantrolene bolus (see below)
4
Hyperventilate: 100% O2 at high flows
5
Apply charcoal filters (Replace q1 hour)
6
Cooling: Lavage cavities / Chilled IV fluids
7
Treat Acidosis (Bicarb) / Hyperkalemia / Arrhythmias
8
Check ABG, Electrolytes, Sugar
9
UOP goal > 1 mL/kg/hr
10
Monitor vitals / Consider central line

Dantrolene Dosing (Actual Weight)

2.5 mg/kg IV bolus q 5-10 min until symptoms abate (max usually 10 mg/kg).

CONTRAINDICATION: Do NOT initiate/administer Calcium Channel Blockers during treatment (Risk of lethal Hyperkalemia/Myocardial depression).
Clinical Note: While CCBs should be avoided during treatment, Dantrolene must still be administered if a patient was already receiving concurrent CCBs prior to the crisis.
Reconstitution:
  • Dantrium/Revonto: 20 mg vial + 60 mL sterile water (Vigorous mixing).
  • Ryanodex: 250 mg vial + 5 mL sterile water.
