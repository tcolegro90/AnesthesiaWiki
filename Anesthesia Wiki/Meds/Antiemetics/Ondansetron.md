# Ondansetron (Zofran)

## Quick Facts
- Generic: Ondansetron hydrochloride
- Brand: Zofran
- Class: Selective 5-HT₃ (serotonin) receptor antagonist / antiemetic
- Controlled substance: No
- Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Indication-Specific Guidance

### 1) PONV Prophylaxis
#### Typical Dosing
- 4 mg IV at end of surgery (most commonly used timing)
#### Practical Considerations
- First-line antiemetic in most PONV prophylaxis protocols
- Effectiveness is highest when given at the end of surgery rather than induction

### 2) PONV Treatment (Rescue)
#### Typical Dosing
- 1 to 4 mg IV; repeat PRN after 4–6 hours
#### Practical Considerations
- Do not repeat the same drug class if prophylaxis was already given with ondansetron — use a different antiemetic mechanism

### 3) Chemotherapy-Induced Nausea/Vomiting
#### Typical Dosing
- Higher doses per oncology protocol (8–24 mg IV)

## Onset and Duration
- Onset IV: 30 minutes (max antiemetic effect)
- Duration: 4 to 8 hours

## Mechanism of Action
- Selective antagonism of **5-HT₃ (serotonin) receptors** in the gut and CNS (chemoreceptor trigger zone and vagal afferents)

## Contraindications / Avoid
- Known hypersensitivity
- Concomitant use with other QT-prolonging agents (additive QT risk)
- Apomorphine use (severe hypotension)

## Key Warnings and Cautions
- **QT prolongation**: Dose-dependent; avoid in patients with congenital long QT syndrome or on other QT-prolonging drugs
- Serotonin syndrome risk when combined with other serotonergic agents (SSRIs, SNRIs, triptans) — rare but recognized

## Common Adverse Effects
- Headache
- Constipation (dose-dependent)
- Elevated LFTs (transient)
- QT prolongation

## Monitoring
- ECG in patients at risk for QT prolongation
- Clinical reassessment of nausea/vomiting control

## Important Interactions
- Tramadol: ondansetron may reduce analgesic effect of tramadol (both act at 5-HT₃)
- QT-prolonging drugs: additive risk

## Notes for Local Customization
- Often part of a combination PONV protocol with dexamethasone and scopolamine patch.
