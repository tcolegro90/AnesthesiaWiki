# Norepinephrine

## Quick Facts
- Generic: Norepinephrine
- Brand: Levophed
- Class: Catecholamine vasopressor (alpha-1 predominant, beta-1 activity)
- Controlled substance: No
- Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Indication-Specific Guidance

### 1) Vasodilatory Shock / Persistent Perioperative Hypotension
#### Dosing
- 0.02 to 0.3 mcg/kg/min IV infusion (titrate to MAP goal)

## Onset and Duration
- Onset: rapid (1–2 minutes)
- Duration: short; dissipates quickly after infusion adjustment

## Mechanism of Action
- Predominantly alpha-1 agonism increases SVR and MAP
- Beta-1 effects support contractility modestly

## Contraindications / Avoid
- Relative caution in severe mesenteric/peripheral ischemia

## Key Warnings and Cautions
- Can reduce peripheral perfusion at higher doses
- Extravasation causes local ischemia; treat with phentolamine per protocol

## Common Adverse Effects
- Hypertension
- Reflex bradycardia (occasionally)
- Arrhythmias (less than epinephrine)

## Monitoring
- Continuous BP (arterial line preferred)
- End-organ perfusion markers (urine output, lactate)

## Important Interactions
- Additive pressor effects with sympathomimetics

## Notes for Local Customization
- First-line vasopressor in many vasodilatory shock pathways.
