# Ephedrine

## Quick Facts
- Generic: Ephedrine
- Brand: Multiple
- Class: Mixed-acting sympathomimetic (indirect + direct)
- Controlled substance: No
- Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Indication-Specific Guidance

### 1) Perioperative Hypotension with Bradycardia
#### Dosing
- 5 to 10 mg IV bolus, repeat as needed
#### Practical Considerations
- Often raises BP and HR together
- Tachyphylaxis can occur with repeated boluses

## Onset and Duration
- Onset: 1–2 minutes IV
- Duration: 10–60 minutes (variable)

## Mechanism of Action
- Indirectly releases endogenous norepinephrine and has some direct alpha/beta agonist activity

## Contraindications / Avoid
- Relative caution in severe tachyarrhythmias

## Key Warnings and Cautions
- Less effective when catecholamine stores are depleted
- Can increase myocardial oxygen demand via tachycardia

## Common Adverse Effects
- Tachycardia
- Hypertension
- Arrhythmias

## Monitoring
- BP and HR after each bolus

## Important Interactions
- Potentiated by MAO inhibitors and some antidepressants

## Notes for Local Customization
- Useful in neuraxial-induced hypotension when accompanying bradycardia is present.
