Acetaminophen (IV)

Quick Facts

  • Generic: Acetaminophen
  • Brand: Ofirmev
  • Class: Non-opioid analgesic / antipyretic
  • Controlled substance: No
  • Last updated: 2026-04-04

Indication-Specific Guidance

1) Perioperative Analgesia (Multimodal)

Dosing

  • 15 mg/kg IV every 6 hours
  • Max single dose: 1000 mg
  • Max total daily dose: 4000 mg (lower institutional limits may apply in hepatic risk)

Practical Considerations

  • Helpful opioid-sparing adjunct
  • Consider lower daily limits in frail patients, malnutrition, chronic alcohol use, or liver disease

Onset and Duration

  • Onset: within 5-10 minutes IV
  • Peak: about 1 hour
  • Duration: 4-6 hours

Mechanism of Action

  • Central analgesic effect via cyclooxygenase pathway modulation and descending serotonergic pathways (exact mechanism incompletely defined)

Contraindications / Avoid

  • Severe hepatic impairment
  • Active severe liver disease
  • Hypersensitivity

Key Warnings and Cautions

  • Hepatotoxicity risk with cumulative overdose
  • Account for all acetaminophen sources (combination products)

Common Adverse Effects

  • Usually well tolerated
  • Rare hypotension (IV use)
  • Elevated liver enzymes with excessive dosing

Monitoring

  • Total daily acetaminophen exposure
  • Liver function in high-risk patients

Important Interactions

  • Chronic high-dose use may affect warfarin INR
