Lidocaine

Quick Facts

  • Generic: Lidocaine
  • Class: Amide local anesthetic
  • Typical concentrations: 0.5%, 1%, 1.5%, 2%, 4%
  • Last updated: 2026-04-04

Indication-Specific Guidance

1) Local Infiltration / Field Block

Typical Dosing

  • Without epinephrine: up to about 4.5 mg/kg (max 300 mg)
  • With epinephrine: up to about 7 mg/kg (max 500 mg)

Practical Considerations

  • Aspirate frequently and inject incrementally.
  • Adjust dose down in elderly/frail or reduced cardiac/hepatic reserve.

2) Peripheral Nerve Block

Typical Dosing

  • Commonly 1% to 2% depending on block and goal.
  • Total mg should remain under weight-based and absolute caps.

Practical Considerations

  • Ultrasound and incremental injection reduce intravascular risk.

3) Epidural (when used as LA)

Typical Dosing

  • Institutional protocol dependent.
  • Track cumulative dose across all boluses.

Practical Considerations

  • Fractionate test and therapeutic doses.

Onset and Duration

  • Onset: rapid
  • Duration: intermediate (longer with epinephrine)

Contraindications / Avoid

  • Known amide local anesthetic allergy (rare)
  • Severe conduction disease without pacing support (systemic exposure concern)

Key Warnings and Cautions

  • Local anesthetic systemic toxicity (LAST)
  • CNS symptoms may precede cardiovascular collapse
  • Lidocaine administered locally near Exparel (liposomal bupivacaine) may cause rapid release of bupivacaine from the liposomal formulation — avoid co-administration at the same site

Monitoring

  • Standard ASA monitors for moderate/large doses
  • Observe for early neurologic or cardiovascular toxicity signs

Notes for Local Customization

  • Verify exact limits against institutional policy.
  • Consider all concurrently administered local anesthetics in cumulative toxicity calculations.
