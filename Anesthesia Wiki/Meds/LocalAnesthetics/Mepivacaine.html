Mepivacaine

Quick Facts

  • Generic: Mepivacaine
  • Class: Amide local anesthetic
  • Typical concentrations: 1%, 1.5%, 2%, 3%
  • Last updated: 2026-04-04

Indication-Specific Guidance

1) Local Infiltration / Field Block

Typical Dosing

  • Without epinephrine: up to about 4.5 mg/kg (max 300 mg)
  • With epinephrine: up to about 7 mg/kg (max 500 mg)

Practical Considerations

  • Intermediate duration option when rapid turnover is desired.

2) Peripheral Nerve Block

Typical Dosing

  • Often 1% to 2% depending on block target and desired duration.
  • Respect cumulative mg limits and mixed-agent contributions.

Practical Considerations

  • Incremental injection and aspiration remain essential.

3) Epidural (select workflows)

Typical Dosing

  • Protocol dependent for loading and maintenance.

Practical Considerations

  • Dose conservatively in reduced clearance states.

Onset and Duration

  • Onset: relatively rapid
  • Duration: intermediate

Contraindications / Avoid

  • Known amide local anesthetic allergy (rare)

Key Warnings and Cautions

  • LAST risk with large cumulative dosing
  • Avoid dose stacking across multiple blocks without recalculation

Monitoring

  • Standard monitoring when moderate/high total doses are used

Notes for Local Customization

  • Verify max dose policy in your institution before clinical use.
