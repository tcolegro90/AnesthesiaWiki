# Tetracaine

## Quick Facts
- Generic: Tetracaine
- Brand: Pontocaine (legacy), others
- Class: Ester local anesthetic
- Controlled substance: No
- Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Indication-Specific Guidance

### 1) Spinal Anesthesia (selected practice settings)
#### Dosing
- Institution-specific spinal dosing depending on concentration/formulation
#### Practical Considerations
- Potent, long-acting ester local anesthetic
- Use preservative-free formulations for neuraxial techniques

### 2) Topical Mucosal/Surface Anesthesia
#### Dosing
- Product-specific topical dosing

## Onset and Duration
- Onset: moderate
- Duration: long (compared with many other ester local anesthetics)

## Mechanism of Action
- Blocks voltage-gated sodium channels in neuronal membranes, preventing propagation of action potentials

## Contraindications / Avoid
- Ester local anesthetic allergy (PABA-related)
- Avoid non-preservative-free products for neuraxial routes

## Key Warnings and Cautions
- Systemic local anesthetic toxicity risk with excessive dosing or intravascular uptake
- Neuraxial use requires correct product selection (preservative-free)

## Common Adverse Effects
- Hypotension (neuraxial sympathectomy-related)
- Nausea
- Local irritation (topical use)

## Monitoring
- Standard local anesthetic monitoring for toxicity signs
- Hemodynamics when used neuraxially

## Important Interactions
- Additive CNS/cardiac toxicity with other local anesthetics if cumulative dosing is high

## Notes for Local Customization
- Legacy wiki listed tetracaine as an ester local anesthetic metabolized by pseudocholinesterase.
