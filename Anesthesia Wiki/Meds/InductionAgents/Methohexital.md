# Methohexital (Brevital)

## Quick Facts
- Generic: Methohexital sodium
- Brand: Brevital
- Class: Ultra-short acting barbiturate
- Controlled substance: Yes (Schedule IV in the US)
- Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Indication-Specific Guidance

### 1) Electroconvulsive Therapy (ECT) Induction
#### Typical Dosing
- 0.75 to 1 mg/kg IV
#### Practical Considerations
- **Gold standard induction agent for ECT** — lowers seizure threshold less than propofol, allowing shorter but adequate seizure duration
- Short duration (8 to 10 minutes) allows for quick recovery between treatments

### 2) Brief Procedural Sedation / Short Induction
#### Typical Dosing
- 1 to 2 mg/kg IV for induction
#### Practical Considerations
- Shorter duration than thiopental
- Rarely used outside ECT in modern practice

## Onset and Duration
- Onset: less than 1 minute IV
- Duration: 8 to 10 minutes

## Mechanism of Action
- Barbiturate — enhances GABA-A receptor chloride conductance (binds at a distinct site from benzodiazepines)

## Contraindications / Avoid
- **Acute Intermittent Porphyria (AIP)**: Barbiturates are absolutely contraindicated — they induce ALA synthase and can precipitate a life-threatening porphyric crisis
- Known barbiturate hypersensitivity
- Status asthmaticus (histamine release risk)

## Key Warnings and Cautions
- Myoclonus and involuntary movements on induction (can mimic seizure)
- Respiratory depression and apnea at higher doses
- Hypotension (less so than thiopental but more than etomidate)
- Administer through a secure, freely flowing IV — extravasation can cause tissue necrosis

## Common Adverse Effects
- Myoclonus
- Apnea / respiratory depression
- Hiccups
- Hypotension

## Monitoring
- Airway, ventilation, and blood pressure post-induction

## Preparation and Administration Notes
- Typically reconstituted to 10 mg/mL (1%) for ECT use
- Keep refrigerated; solution is alkaline — incompatible with acidic solutions in same line

## Reversal / Overdose Strategy
- No specific reversal agent
- Support airway, ventilation, and hemodynamics

## Special Populations
- Porphyria: **Absolutely contraindicated**

## Notes for Local Customization
- In ECT settings, dose is titrated based on anesthesia team's seizure monitoring.
