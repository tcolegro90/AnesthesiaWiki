# Dantrolene

## Quick Facts
- Generic: Dantrolene
- Brand: Ryanodex, Revonto, Dantrium
- Class: Skeletal muscle relaxant (ryanodine receptor antagonist)
- Controlled substance: No
- Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Indication-Specific Guidance

### 1) Malignant Hyperthermia (MH) Crisis
#### Dosing
- Initial dose: 2.5 mg/kg IV
- Repeat 1 mg/kg to 2.5 mg/kg as needed until signs improve
- Continue post-crisis dosing per protocol (e.g., 1 mg/kg every 4-6 hours or infusion)
#### Practical Considerations
- Administer early when MH suspected; do not delay for confirmatory testing
- Use with aggressive cooling, correction of acidosis/hyperkalemia, and ICU-level monitoring

## Onset and Duration
- Onset: minutes after IV administration
- Duration: variable; maintenance therapy usually required for 24-48 hours due to recrudescence risk

## Mechanism of Action
- Inhibits abnormal calcium release from sarcoplasmic reticulum via ryanodine receptor (RYR1), reducing hypermetabolic muscle activity

## Contraindications / Avoid
- No absolute contraindication in life-threatening MH crisis

## Key Warnings and Cautions
- **Do not use calcium channel blockers with dantrolene in MH treatment** (risk of severe hyperkalemia and cardiovascular collapse)
- Reconstitution logistics vary by formulation; ensure MH cart readiness

## Common Adverse Effects
- Muscle weakness
- Sedation
- Phlebitis with IV use
- Transient liver enzyme elevation (especially with prolonged therapy)

## Monitoring
- Core temperature
- ETCO2 trend
- CK, potassium, blood gas, lactate
- Urine output and renal function

## Important Interactions
- Calcium channel blockers during MH management should be avoided

## Notes for Local Customization
- Keep dosing cards and reconstitution instructions with the MH cart for rapid use.
