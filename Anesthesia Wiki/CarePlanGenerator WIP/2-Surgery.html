ASA Physical Status Classifications
ClassDescription
ASA INormal healthy patient — non-smoker, no or minimal alcohol use.
ASA IIMild systemic disease — e.g., social drinker, active smoker, pregnancy, obesity (BMI 30–40), well-controlled DM or HTN.
ASA IIISevere systemic disease — e.g., BMI ≥40, poorly controlled DM or HTN, stable angina, h/o MI or CVA >3 months ago, ESRD on dialysis.
ASA IVSevere systemic disease — constant threat to life. E.g., recent MI or CVA <3 months, unstable angina, severe valve dysfunction, sepsis.
ASA VMoribund — not expected to survive without surgery. E.g., ruptured aneurysm, massive trauma.
ASA VIBrain-dead patient awaiting organ procurement.

Append E for emergency procedures (e.g., ASA IIE).
