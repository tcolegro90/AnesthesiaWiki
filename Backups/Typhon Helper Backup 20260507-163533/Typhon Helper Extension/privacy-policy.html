Privacy Policy

Typhon Case Helper Chrome Extension  ·  Last updated: April 20, 2026

Overview

Typhon Case Helper is a Chrome extension designed to help student nurse anesthetists (SRNAs) quickly log clinical cases and time entries on the Typhon NAST platform. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

Data We Collect

  • Authentication information — your email address and password (via Firebase Authentication) or Google account credentials, used solely to sign you in and sync your data across devices.
  • Clinical case log data — case details you enter into the extension, including clinical site, ASA physical status, biological sex, procedure types, and anesthesia technique. This data belongs to you and is stored to pre-fill Typhon forms.

How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and associate your saved case data with your account.
  • To sync your case log entries across devices via Firebase Firestore.
  • To auto-fill Typhon NAST case log and time log entry forms on your behalf.

Your data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose outside of the extension's core function.

Data Storage

Case data is stored locally using chrome.storage.local on your device. If you sign in, your data is additionally synced to Google Firebase Firestore (hosted in the United States) under your user account. Firebase's privacy practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google OAuth / Authentication Bridge

Google sign-in is facilitated through an intermediate page hosted at courtstatus.gear.host. This page handles only the OAuth token handoff and does not store any credentials or personal data.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your locally stored case data at any time from within the extension. To delete your cloud-synced data or account, contact the developer at the email below and your Firestore data will be removed within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

This extension is intended for use by adult healthcare students and professionals. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
