Style 2 — Tooltip (hover on desktop)

Hovering over "?" shows a floating bubble with a short COA excerpt. No click needed.

Pain
One-on-one patient interaction to intervene in acute or chronic pain. Must include patient assessment before the action.

Examples: epidural/intrathecal initiation, PCA start, peripheral nerve block for post-op pain, PACU pain management.

⚠️ A primary spinal/epidural anesthetic does NOT count.
COA Guidelines — Pain Management Encounters (min. 15)
CVC
Insertion of a central venous catheter. The placement of an introducer qualifies. If you also float a PA catheter, count both separately. Student must perform the insertion.
COA Guidelines — CVC Insertion (min. 10 actual)
⚠️ PICC insertion does NOT meet the CVC Placement requirement. Tracked separately.
COA Guidelines — Central Venous Catheter Insertion
Airway
Placement of a tracheal tube using a flexible endoscope (fiberoptic bronchoscope or flexible videoscope).

Sim may satisfy part but not all of the 5 required endoscopic techniques.
COA Guidelines — Endoscopic Techniques (min. 5 total)
Airway assessment via flexible endoscopic bronchoscopy to evaluate airway patency and/or ventilatory mechanics.

⚠️ Assessment via an in-situ ETT does NOT count toward the 5 required endoscopic techniques.
COA Guidelines — Endoscopic Techniques
Style 3 — Inline Expand

Clicking "?" toggles a panel that opens directly below the item. No overlay — stays in context.

Regional Techniques
Minimum: 10 spinal regional techniques required. Can be for anesthesia or pain management purposes.

As long as students administer no fewer than 10 spinals, 10 epidurals, and 10 peripheral blocks, the remaining 5 additional required techniques can be any combination.
COA Guidelines — Regional Techniques, Spinal (min. 10)
Minimum: 10 epidural regional techniques required. Can be for anesthesia or pain management.

When a student performs a combined spinal/epidural (CSE), both procedures count — count as both a spinal AND an epidural.
COA Guidelines — Regional Techniques, Epidural (min. 10)
Minimum: 10 peripheral blocks required (upper or lower extremity, and/or pain management).

Includes truncal blocks (e.g., TAP, rectus sheath, ilioinguinal, iliohypogastric), head/neck blocks, and oral/maxillofacial blocks.

⚠️ Simulation cannot be used to meet the required number of peripheral or neuraxial blocks.
COA Guidelines — Regional Techniques, Peripheral (min. 10)
Students may count experiences in this category only if the procedure being performed is intended to facilitate delivery of the fetus.

Minimums: 30 total OB management cases, including at least 10 cesarean deliveries and 10 labor analgesia cases.

Example: A student with 60 OB cases but only 8 C-sections still fails requirements — they need 2 more C-sections.
COA Guidelines — Obstetrical Management (min. 30 total; 10 C/S; 10 labor)
